Programmes & Events

How YNAN brings neuroscience to young people across Nigeria, through national programmes and chapter activities.

Brain Awareness Week

Joining the global campaign each year with school outreaches, seminars and public talks on brain and mental health.

Brain Bee

Preparing high school students for the annual Brain Bee competition and eligibility for international rounds.

Monthly Online Workshops

Regular virtual workshops open to all members across chapters.

Lecture Series & Webinars

Talks from neuroscientists and neuro-practitioners, plus online courses for members.

Journal Club

Reading and discussing neuroscience research in an informal setting, without the pressure of being graded.

NeuroSchool & NeuroClubs

Bringing neuroscience to primary and high school students through NeuroSchool and school NeuroClubs.

Community Outreach

Raising awareness of neurological conditions and how to deal with them.

Conferences & Publications

Support to present papers at local and international conferences (e.g. NSN) and publish review articles, with travel grants for outstanding members.

Awards

ECO awards for outstanding students and the Outstanding Member Award for exceptionally active members.

Chapters also run their own yearly projects alongside these national programmes.
