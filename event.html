YNAN Initiative

Climate and the Brain Africa (CBA)

Connecting Climate Action, Neuroscience and Community Health.

An initiative led by the Youth Neuroscience Association of Nigeria (YNAN) to promote awareness of the impact of climate change on brain health.

Climate and Brain Awareness01

Educating young people on climate change and brain health.

Youth Ambassadors02

Training young climate and neuroscience advocates.

Community Engagement03

Taking climate and brain-health education to schools and communities.

Citizen Science04

Engaging youth in environmental and cognitive wellbeing research.

Climate Action05

Promoting community-led solutions to heat exposure, flooding and pollution.

Our vision

Empowering African youth to connect neuroscience, environmental sustainability and community health.

Neuroscience competitions for high school students

Brain Bee Programme

We organise and mentor high school students for Brain Bee neuroscience competitions, including Nigeria's first national Brain Bee.

  • Hundreds of students inspired toward neuroscience careers
  • Participants progressing into YNAN leadership roles
  • Early talent identification and development

Early-stage research for undergraduates

International Neuronexus Symposium & Proposal Award

Nigerian and international students are trained in research proposal development, peer review and scientific presentation, and present their ideas to a global audience.

  • Outstanding proposals recognised with awards
  • Finalists from Nigeria, Tanzania and India
  • Young neuroscientists ready for global engagement
