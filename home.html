You are. Welcome to

The Youth Neuroscience Association of Nigeria (YNAN) empowers primary school, high school and undergraduate students to pursue careers in neuroscience.

What We Do

Ensuring no passionate mind is left behind

Mentorship

Connecting young people with mentors, research fellowships and professional networks in neuroscience.

Education & Training

Monthly online workshops, lecture series, webinars, online courses, a journal club and NeuroSchool.

Research & Publications

Supporting paper presentations at local and international conferences and review articles in high-impact journals.

Advocacy & Awareness

Outreaches, Brain Awareness Week and Brain Bee programmes that raise awareness of neurological conditions.

Our Activities

BRAIN AWARENESS WEEK CAMPAIGN

We join the global community with Dana Foundation to promote the importance of the brain and and Mental health with:

  • School outreaches, seminars & talks
  • Public awareness on neurological conditions
  • Brain health & mental health education
  • Youth-led Brain Awareness Week events
Our Activities

BRAINBEE COMPETITIONS

With our organization’s mission always in mind, we strive every year to sensitize and prepare high school students for the annual brainbee competitions for eligibility at international competitions.

How To Get Involved

Join YNAN, grow with a chapter and get recognised for your impact

1

Become a Member

Open to undergraduates in neuroscience-related fields, recent graduates and neuroscience enthusiasts. There is no membership fee.

2

Join or Start a Chapter

Take part in chapter meetings, workshops and yearly projects, or start a chapter at your university, polytechnic or college of education.

3

Lead & Be Recognised

2019

The year YNAN was founded.

“Ma, can you train us?”

A question from a high school student during a 2018 TReND for Africa outreach inspired Dr. Chinna Orish to establish YNAN in 2019, so that young Nigerians passionate about the brain would have the mentorship, resources and guidance to pursue neuroscience.
