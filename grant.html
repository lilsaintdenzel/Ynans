Outreach Grant Application

YNAN Call for Outreach Grant: apply on behalf of your chapter to run a neuroclimate and brain health outreach.

Theme

“Neuroclimate and Brain Health for the Youth: Understanding How Climate Change Affects the Brain.”

₦50,000 per successful chapter
  • Eligibility: active YNAN chapters in Nigerian universities and communities.
  • Supported activities: school outreach, public lectures, social media campaigns, brain health walks, environmental awareness campaigns and interactive workshops on climate change and brain health.
  • Your application should cover: the proposed activity, target audience, expected impact, implementation plan and budget.
  • Follow-up award: the chapter with the most impactful outreach receives the YNAN Neuroclimate Brain Awareness Champion Award.
