2.0 Code of Conduct

Our values and expectations

Values & Principles

  • Respect: treat others with respect, kindness and compassion, regardless of background, culture or opinions.
  • Integrity: act with honesty, transparency and accountability.
  • Professionalism: maintain a professional demeanor at all YNAN events, meetings and communications.
  • Inclusivity: promote diversity, equity and inclusion in all activities and decisions.
  • Responsibility: take ownership of actions and decisions and be accountable for their consequences.

Behavioral Expectations

  • Punctuality & attendance: attend events and meetings regularly and on time.
  • Active participation: engage in discussions and support YNAN's growth. Failure to participate in meetings regularly may result in removal from membership.
  • Respectful communication: avoid discriminatory, harassing or unprofessional language and behavior.
  • Confidentiality: protect sensitive or personal information shared within YNAN.

Social Media & Online Presence

  • Keep your online presence respectful, professional and consistent with YNAN's values.
  • All members are required to have an active LinkedIn account, YNAN's primary platform for networking and updates.
  • Engage with YNAN posts on LinkedIn by liking, commenting and sharing.
  • Avoid posts that may reflect negatively on YNAN or its members.
  • Never share confidential or sensitive information online.
3.0 Membership

Becoming a YNAN member

Who is eligible?

  • Undergraduate students studying Anatomy, Physiology, Biochemistry, Computer Science, Psychology, Medicine, Nursing and other health-related professions.
  • Recent graduates (within 2–3 years of graduation) in fields related to neuroscience.
  • Neuroscience enthusiasts, including professionals, researchers, educators and anyone with a strong interest in neuroscience.

YNAN does not charge a statutory payment or membership fee.

Membership benefits

  • Access to exclusive events such as conferences, seminars and workshops
  • Networking with professionals and peers in neuroscience
  • Educational resources and opportunities:
    • Award of excellence and travel grants to local conferences (e.g. NSN)
    • Research fellowship opportunities
    • Mentorship opportunities
    • Online courses and webinars
    • Publications
  • Leadership development and skill-building
  • Discounts on neuroscience events, conferences and publications

Member responsibilities

  • Attend all regular meetings: chapter meetings, monthly online workshops and the lecture series (attendance is recorded)
  • Participate in discussions and contribute to YNAN's growth
  • Volunteer for committees or tasks that support YNAN's mission
  • Maintain a professional and respectful demeanor
  • Adhere to YNAN's code of conduct and values
4.0 Governance & Leadership

How YNAN is led

Organizational Structure

  • National Executives: provide overall leadership and direction to YNAN.
  • Supervisory Body (chaired by the National Chapter Coordinator): coordinates and forms chapters, supervises all YNAN activities and ensures compliance with policies and guidelines.
  • Chapter Executives: manage individual chapters, implement YNAN's mission locally, organize chapter events and report to the Supervisory Body and National Executives.

Leadership eligibility

  • Membership: registered YNAN members with at least 2 years of membership for national roles, or 1 year for chapter roles.
  • Academic status: undergraduate or graduate student in a Nigerian institution.
  • Age: between 18 and 28.

President

Responsibilities

  • Provides overall leadership and direction to YNAN
  • Sets the organization's vision and strategy
  • Oversees the implementation of YNAN's projects and programs
  • Represents YNAN in external forums and events
  • Ensures effective communication among team members and stakeholders
  • Facilitates chapter creation
  • Performs all roles and responsibilities administered by the Patron

What we look for

  • Previous leadership experience in a student organisation or academic setting
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills
  • A clear vision for the organisation's growth and development
  • Ability to commit at least 10 hours per week

Vice-President / Academic Coordinator

Responsibilities

  • Supports the President and assumes leadership in their absence
  • Coordinates academic events, workshops and conferences
  • Develops and implements academic programs and initiatives
  • Collaborates with universities and research institutions
  • Assists in setting the organization's strategic direction

What we look for

  • Ability to support the President and lead in their absence
  • Strong knowledge of neuroscience and academic programs
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills
  • Ability to coordinate events, workshops and NeuroSchool
  • Ability to work with universities and research institutions

Secretary General

Responsibilities

  • Record-keeping, correspondence and documentation
  • Maintains accurate, up-to-date records of meetings, decisions and actions
  • Prepares and distributes meeting minutes and reports
  • Handles communication with external parties
  • Ensures compliance with organizational policies and procedures

What we look for

  • Ability to maintain accurate records
  • Excellent written and verbal communication
  • Attention to detail in minutes and reports
  • Strong time management
  • Ability to handle sensitive information confidentially

Editor-in-Chief

Responsibilities

  • Oversees YNAN's newsletter, blog and other publications
  • Coordinates content creation, editing and proofreading
  • Ensures high-quality content aligned with YNAN's mission
  • Collaborates with writers, designers and other stakeholders
  • Develops a content strategy that engages YNAN's audience
  • Ensures papers sent for conferences are reviewed

What we look for

  • Strong writing and editing skills
  • Strong knowledge of neuroscience-related topics
  • Excellent communication skills
  • Attention to detail
  • Creativity in developing content ideas and strategies

Public Relations Officer / Social Media Manager

Responsibilities

  • Handles communication, publicity and media relations
  • Develops and implements a social media strategy
  • Creates and curates content for social media platforms
  • Collaborates with media outlets, influencers and stakeholders
  • Ensures effective communication and reputation management

What we look for

  • Excellent written and verbal communication
  • Ability to develop social media strategies
  • Creativity in content and campaigns
  • Attention to detail
  • Ability to analyse social media metrics and adjust strategies

Membership Coordinator

Responsibilities

  • Develops membership recruitment and retention strategies
  • Coordinates membership events, workshops and activities
  • Ensures effective communication with members
  • Develops membership programs with the leadership team
  • Maintains accurate membership records

What we look for

  • Excellent interpersonal skills
  • Ability to develop recruitment strategies
  • Strong organisational and record-keeping skills
  • Attention to detail with membership data
  • Excellent customer service to members

Projects Coordinator(s)

Responsibilities

  • Oversees YNAN's projects and programs
  • Coordinates NeuroClubs
  • Develops project plans, timelines and budgets
  • Coordinates project teams, stakeholders and partners
  • Ensures effective project monitoring, evaluation and reporting
  • Develops project strategies with the leadership team

What we look for

  • Project management skills
  • Ability to coordinate teams and stakeholders
  • Excellent communication skills
  • Attention to detail with project data
  • Strong problem-solving and decision-making

How to apply

  • Completed application form
  • Up-to-date CV
  • A 500-word personal statement outlining your vision, experience and qualifications for the position
  • Two recommendation letters from academic or professional referees
  • Academic transcript

Roles are subject to modification by the Patron of YNAN.

Selection process

  • Application review by the executive committee.
  • Interviews for shortlisted candidates.
  • Appointment & probation: successful candidates serve an 8-week probation to confirm effectiveness and fit for the post.

Chapter leadership

Selection

  • Guided and supervised by the National Executive Council.
  • Candidates must meet national criteria on age, membership status and leadership experience.
  • A panel of National (Supervisory) representatives and chapter members interviews and assesses candidates.

Term & accountability

  • Chapter executives serve a fixed 1-year term and ensure a smooth handover.
  • Regular reports to the National body on activities, progress and challenges.
  • An Annual General Meeting where executives report to chapter members.
5.0 – 6.0 Chapters

Chapter expectations & starting a new chapter

What every chapter must do

  • Regular meetings on dates set by the chapter, with the National Executive Council informed and minutes taken.
  • Monthly report on all activities (meetings, outreaches, collaborations), submitted on or before the 5th of the new month, including meeting minutes and the chapter's strengths, weaknesses and problems.
  • Statutory meetings: the chapter President, Vice President and Secretary must attend all statutory meetings called by the National Executive Council or the Patron.
  • Yearly projects independent of national projects, registered with the National Executive Council.
  • Membership data sent regularly: numerical strength, level and discipline of members, and activeness of individuals.
  • Event participation: at least 10 members present at any YNAN workshop or event.

Starting a new chapter

Eligibility

  • Located in a Nigerian university, polytechnic or college of education.
  • Demonstrated interest in neuroscience among students at that institution.

Organizational requirements

  • An executive board of at least 5: President, Vice-President/Academic Coordinator, Secretary, PRO/Social Media Manager and Projects Coordinator.
  • Willingness to adopt and follow the YNAN guidelines.
  • At least 10 registered members from different levels and neuroscience-related departments.

Applying & approval

Submit

  • An application form signed by the proposed executive board.
  • A detailed proposal on how the chapter will contribute to YNAN's growth: goals, objectives and planned activities.

Review

  • Reviewed by the YNAN National Executive Council.
  • A site visit may be conducted to verify the information.
  • Approved or rejected by the Supervisory Body.

After approval

  • Register with the YNAN National Secretariat.
  • Submit monthly chapter reports and an annual report to the National Secretariat.
  • Comply with all YNAN policies and guidelines.
  • Collect your certificate of membership from the National Secretariat.

7.0 – 8.0 Finance & Recognition

Finance, points and rewards

Finance

₦0

As a non-profit, YNAN does not have a statutory payment or membership fee.

Any financial needs or requests must be communicated to the National Executive Council for approval and guidance.

Chapter point system

The Supervisory Body grades chapters on achievement and compliance.

Points are earned for

  • Achieving set goals and objectives
  • Successful events and activities
  • Timely, accurate reports
  • Active participation and engagement

Points are deducted for

  • Non-compliance with YNAN rules and guidelines
  • Inactive participation
  • Poor reporting and communication
  • Not achieving set goals

The chapter with the lowest points at year end is placed on suspension; failure to improve during suspension leads to disqualification from YNAN.

Rewards

High-performing chapters

  • Recognition at YNAN events and on social media
  • Additional support and resources
  • Leadership opportunities for members, such as serving on the National Executive Council or leading national projects

Exceptionally active individuals

  • The Outstanding Member Award
  • Leadership development programs, workshops and conferences
  • National recognition, including on YNAN's website and social media
